Spacex

SpaceX & Nvidia Partner on Orbital AI Data Centers

SpaceX announced it will build its Starmind space-based data centers around Nvidia's Vera Rubin NVL72 rack, with Elon Musk targeting Q4 2027 for the first orbital deployment. The space-optimized rack is described as simpler, lighter, and denser than typical hardware, tweaked for radiation and heat conditions in orbit. Analysts currently estimate orbital compute costs at 4x ground compute, though Musk claims this gap will flip in coming years.

Each Vera Rubin NVL72 rack packs 72 chips working as one computer, with each chip delivering up to 25x the computing power of the older H100.

Thomson Reuters Builds Proprietary Legal AI Model

Thomson Reuters unveiled its first in-house AI model, built by fine-tuning Alibaba's open-source Qwen model on decades of proprietary legal content from Westlaw and other sources. The two-year, $40M project yielded a model the company internally benchmarks ahead of Claude Opus 4.8, Gemini 3.1 Pro, and GPT-5.5 in certain legal tasks. An open-weights version will be released for researchers, though the model has only been tested internally so far.

The $40M development effort included a training run costing $450K, with the model covering less than 10% of Thomson Reuters' overall content library so far.
