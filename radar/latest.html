OpenAI's 'Astra' Solves 10 Long-Open Math Problems

OpenAI revealed that Astra, an internal next-generation model, solved 10 long-standing open problems in math and computer science, including one nearly 30 years old, spanning geometry, group theory, and quantum complexity. China Fires Up World's Largest Superconducting Magnet

China's CRAFT fusion project successfully tested a 582-tonne toroidal-field superconducting magnet in Hefei, the world's largest of its kind, capable of confining plasma at 100 million degrees Celsius. Sixteen of these magnets will form a ring as a critical step toward a working fusion power plant. The milestone gives China a significant lead in the global race toward commercial nuclear fusion.

#10

AI-Engineered Enzyme Reverses Key Aging Driver

Scientists used AI to identify and engineer CMLase, a natural enzyme that removes advanced glycation end-products (AGEs) — sticky, inflammatory molecules that accumulate as we age and contribute to heart disease, kidney failure, and eye damage. In one test, the enzyme restored 70-year-old human skin to AGE levels typical of a 30-year-old. The breakthrough represents a major milestone in AI-assisted anti-aging research.

CMLase restored 70-year-old human skin to AGE levels typical of a 30-year-old in lab tests
