OpenAI's Jalapeño Chip Beats Nvidia on Performance

OpenAI revealed its first custom AI inference chip, Jalapeño, built in partnership with Broadcom, at the Hot Chips conference. The chip went from concept to working silicon in under two years, with OpenAI's own AI models accelerating the design process.
