#3
Anthropic

Anthropic unveils FlashProve provable inference system

Anthropic published a blog post introducing FlashProve, a system for what it calls provable inference, which applies zero-trust security principles to AI model outputs. The idea is that enterprises can cryptographically verify that a model actually produced a given output, rather than taking it on faith. It's a direct response to the trust problem that's been slowing enterprise AI adoption, particularly in regulated industries where auditability isn't optional.

FlashProve lets enterprises cryptographically verify AI model outputs rather than relying on the provider's word.
