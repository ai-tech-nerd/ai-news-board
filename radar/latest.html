#13
Uc berkeley / nvidia

AI agents could close robotics' 100,000-year data gap

Research from UC Berkeley and Nvidia proposes using AI agents in the design, testing, and control of robots as a way to close what the researchers call a "100,000-year data gap" — the massive shortfall between the training data robots need and what currently exists. Rather than waiting for real-world data to accumulate, agents would generate, evaluate, and iterate on robotic behavior synthetically. The approach could dramatically accelerate how quickly robots become reliable in unstructured environments.

Researchers describe a "100,000-year data gap" between what robots need to learn reliably and the training data that currently exists.
