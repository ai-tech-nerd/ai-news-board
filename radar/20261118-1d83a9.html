US moves to cut China's remote access to AI chips

The US government is closing a loophole that let foreign buyers — including Chinese firms — access US AI chips remotely through data centers located overseas. Under the old setup, buyers could use the hardware without physically importing it, effectively sidestepping export controls. The move follows the investigation into whether Nvidia chips were diverted to China and signals the administration is tightening enforcement across the board.

Foreign buyers were able to run US AI hardware through overseas data centers without importing it, bypassing export controls entirely until this rule change.
