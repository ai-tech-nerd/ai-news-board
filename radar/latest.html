Anthropic: AI amplifies workers, doesn't replace them

Anthropic interviewed 58 of its US enterprise customers across roughly 300 AI use cases and found that the biggest gains came from using AI as a force multiplier for existing staff, not from cutting headcount. The main bottlenecks are employee resistance and task categories, like code review and sales, that AI hasn't been able to accelerate yet. The report says the most value comes from companies that completely reorganize job roles around AI rather than bolting it onto existing workflows.

AI is slowing hiring and reducing contractor use, particularly offshore contractors, but hasn't caused significant layoffs.
