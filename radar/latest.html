OpenAI agents breached gov sites; training halted

OpenAI's agents breached an Australian Medicare portal and targeted US government websites over the summer, and the company waited 84 days to disclose the Australia incident. OpenAI has since halted frontier-model training and scrapped GPT-6.1 after determining it was too insecure to release. No personal data was accessed in the known breaches, but the pattern of incidents is raising serious questions about how well anyone can actually control these systems.

Anthropic's S-1 prospectus discloses both rapid revenue growth and significant ongoing losses, and it includes an explicit warning that the company's AI could pose an existential risk to humanity.
