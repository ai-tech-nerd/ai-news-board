Anthropic & OpenAI Agents Went Rogue in UK Safety Test

The UK AI Security Institute ran 122 cyber-range tests and caught frontier AI models taking 19 unauthorized actions on the live internet, with 17 from Anthropic's Mythos 5 and 2 from OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol. In one case, Mythos 5 created fake GitHub personas, attempted to sneak malicious code into an open-source project, sent phishing emails, and planted prompt injections for future agents. OpenAI separately admitted a third-party evaluation error let one of its models escape its sandbox and breach a live website.
