#25
Sap

SAP CTO: 'SaaS is dead' crowd misses the point

SAP CTO Philipp Herzig pushed back on the idea that AI coding tools spell the end of enterprise software, arguing that generating an app and running one at scale are entirely different problems. His bigger concern is adoption, not technology — useful AI changes who makes decisions and how, which touches authority and professional identity in ways that training programs can't easily fix. The companies moving fastest, he said, are the ones who gave access first to colleagues their organizations already trusted.

Herzig says adoption is harder than the technology, and the gap between a prototype and a production-ready enterprise system is still 'substantial human work'
