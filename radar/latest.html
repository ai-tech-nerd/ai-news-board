Anthropic co-founder Jack Clark has moved his prediction for fully automated AI research, known as recursive self-improvement, from 2028 to 2027. 400+ researchers demand stronger AI containment

More than 400 researchers, including AI godfathers Yoshua Bengio and Geoffrey Hinton, published an open letter calling for immediate action on AI containment.
