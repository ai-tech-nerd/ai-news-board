#2
Anthropic

Claude helps prove Riemann Hypothesis after 167 years

Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpöge used Claude to produce a formal proof of the Riemann Hypothesis, one of math's most famous unsolved problems, after roughly six weeks of work. The proof runs 17 million lines of Lean code and has been certified correct by the Lean and Mathlib communities. Critics from the Association for Human Mathematics say no human can actually follow the reasoning, but the formal verification checks out.

The proof spans 17 million lines of machine-checkable Lean code and carries a $1 million Clay Millennium Prize.
