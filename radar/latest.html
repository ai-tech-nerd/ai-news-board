OpenAI safety lead quits, calls culture 'broken'

David Robinson spent 3.5 years at OpenAI overseeing safety reports for 12 frontier model launches and drafting the company's Preparedness Framework. He resigned and published an essay in The Atlantic calling the culture 'broken,' saying labs should operate more like nuclear plants and airports, with layered redundancy rather than nonstop sprinting. His exit follows the firing of three other researchers who reportedly shared sensitive information with an outside safety group.

Robinson oversaw safety reports for 12 frontier model launches before quitting.

#5

MCP protocol has a structural flaw in agent comms

A vulnerability discovered in agents from Google and others reveals a systemic risk in the Model Context Protocol, the emerging standard for agent-to-agent communication. The flaw isn't a one-off bug — it points to a structural problem in how MCP handles trust between agents. As MCP adoption grows, so does the attack surface this weakness creates.

The vulnerability affects agents from Google and multiple other vendors, indicating a protocol-level flaw rather than an isolated implementation bug.

#13
Black forest labs

FLUX 3 Image gets wider release with bounding-box layout control

Black Forest Labs has broadly released FLUX 3 Image, the multimodal model that originally launched in July. The standout feature is bounding-box control: users draw a box for each element they want in the image, describe what goes in each box, and the model builds the final composition around that layout. It gives designers and creators precise spatial control that most image generators don't offer.

FLUX 3 Image uses spatial bounding boxes to let users define exact placement of each image element before generation
