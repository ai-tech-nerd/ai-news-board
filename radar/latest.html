Cohere Launches Parse: Niche Vision Model for Enterprise Docs

Cohere debuted Cohere Parse, a vision language model specifically designed to process large volumes of enterprise documents cost-effectively at $1.50 per 1,000 pages, compared to roughly $10 per 1,000 pages for hyperscaler models. Cohere claims Parse offers the best price-performance tradeoff among models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Databricks, Mistral, Microsoft, Google, and Amazon.

FDA Approves Daraxonrasib, Doubling Pancreatic Cancer Survival

The FDA approved daraxonrasib, a twice-daily pill targeting the KRAS protein mutation, which helps advanced pancreatic cancer patients live a median of over 13 months compared to less than 7 months on chemotherapy alone. Pancreatic cancer kills over 50,000 Americans annually and has historically had very poor survival odds. The KRAS protein was previously considered impossible to drug.

Daraxonrasib extends median survival for advanced pancreatic cancer patients from under 7 months to over 13 months

MIT Startup Electra Raises $850M for Short-Haul Hybrid Aircraft

MIT startup Electra unveiled a hybrid aircraft design using eight electric motors and a gas-powered generator that can take off and land in the space of a soccer field, with a 1,200-mile range and 200 mph cruising speed. The aircraft is designed for the underserved 50-to-250-mile regional travel market that's too short to fly but too long to comfortably drive. The company raised $850 million to scale production in Ohio, targeting 800 aircraft per year.

UCSF Maps 100 Proteins Linked to Severe Autism

Researchers at UCSF mapped 100 proteins linked to severe autism and traced over 1,000 proteins they interact with, revealing how genetic mutations disrupt critical molecular partnerships in brain development. In one case, a mutation broke the bond between two proteins, allowing one to interfere with normal brain cell division. The discovery provides concrete drug targets that 20 years of gene research had failed to deliver.
