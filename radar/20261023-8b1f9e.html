AI finds critical PHP flaws affecting 70% of web

Agent payments could drive 'agent-flation'

AI agents that can spend money on behalf of users are raising a new economic concern: that automated purchasing behavior could push consumer prices higher. Visa and Mastercard both introduced agent payment rails in April, and the worry is that agents optimizing for convenience rather than price comparison could reduce the competitive pressure that normally keeps prices in check. Some analysts are calling the potential effect 'agent-flation.'

Visa and Mastercard both launched agent payment infrastructure in April 2026, enabling AI to transact on behalf of users.
