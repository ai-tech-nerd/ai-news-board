Amodei, Altman Call for Slowing AI Development

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei published an essay titled 'We Must Pace the Frontier,' arguing that recursive self-improvement and rogue agent incidents mean safety can't keep up with capability gains. He proposed a three-part framework: embedded third-party evaluators, a democratic lab coalition, and global coordination with authoritarian governments. OpenAI's Sam Altman, Elon Musk, Demis Hassabis, and Satya Nadella all backed the direction, while Trump dismissed slowdown advocates as 'negative forces.'

Amodei warned that within 6-12 months, a swarm of agents could be capable of taking over the entire internet.
