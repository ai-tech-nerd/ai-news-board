#5
Anthropic

Anthropic report: AI could flip the labor/capital split

Anthropic's economics team published research outlining three scenarios for AI's economic impact by 2030. The extreme scenario projects a 32.4% GDP increase but 13.5% of knowledge workers displaced, with only 5.2% finding new work and wages stagnating as the labor-to-capital income split inverts from 60/40 to roughly 40/60. Economists are pushing back on some of the assumptions, particularly whether an economy with that much unemployment and wealth concentration could actually generate the projected GDP growth.

In Anthropic's extreme scenario, 8.3% of knowledge workers would be unemployed by 2030 with no new role found, while GDP surges 32.4% and capital captures a larger share of income than labor for the first time.
