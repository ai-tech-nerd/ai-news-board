#13
Goodfire

Goodfire: AI neurons are readable, not encrypted chaos

Goodfire's new paper 'Neurons as an Interpretable Basis' finds that individual neurons in AI models can be probed and understood through normal scientific methods, without any special decryption step. The finding pushes back on a dominant assumption in the field: that polysemanticity and superposition make neural networks fundamentally hard to decode in practice. Tom McGrath, Goodfire's chief scientist, told The Deep View this means the black box framing is no longer a defensible position.

Goodfire's research shows AI neurons can be probed directly to predict what prompts activate them and how to influence their behavior, with no esoteric decryption required.
