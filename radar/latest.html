Pathway's Post-Transformer Model Challenges AI Economics

AI startup Pathway unveiled BDH-CQ, a 150-million parameter small reasoning model built on a post-transformer architecture that achieves near-frontier reasoning at a fraction of the cost. On the ARC-AGI-1 benchmark, it scored 29.5% pass@2 at just $0.0007 per task — compared to OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Luna which scores 34.5% but costs 11x more. The team includes co-authors of the original Transformer paper and advisors from Google DeepMind and Databricks.

BDH-CQ costs $0.0007 per task on ARC-AGI-1 — 11 times cheaper than OpenAI's most cost-effective GPT-5.6 Luna model.
