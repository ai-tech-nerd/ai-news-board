Typesafe

TypeSafe's Jev: A new class of AI that can't hallucinate

Ex-OpenAI researcher Diogo Almeida launched TypeSafe's Jev from stealth, a new kind of AI model that only chooses between preset options rather than generating free text. It runs at $42 per billion input tokens with free output, and responds in 70-500ms, which TypeSafe claims is 40-200x faster than today's LLMs. Because it only selects from predefined choices, the company says hallucinations are structurally impossible.

Jev costs $42 per billion input tokens with free output, which TypeSafe estimates at 238x cheaper than Claude Fable 5.1.
