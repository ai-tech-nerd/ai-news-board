METR: Anthropic improving models 1.9x faster since Oct 2025

METR published the first high-access independent audit of AI R&D acceleration inside a frontier lab, spending about 2.5 full-time staff on Anthropic's systems from August to late October. The headline finding is that Anthropic's internal capabilities index grew at 1.9x its pre-October 2025 rate, with a 95% confidence interval of 1.5x to 2.8x. Compute scaling didn't accelerate, so METR's rough estimate is that about 80% of the speedup comes from AI doing the R&D work itself.
