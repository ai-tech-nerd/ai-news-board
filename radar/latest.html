#2
Inception

Inception Mercury 3 upends AI economics

Inception launched Mercury 3, a diffusion-based LLM that generates text by refining multiple tokens in parallel rather than one at a time, hitting 1,000 tokens per second and running 4x to 8x faster than comparable models from Anthropic and OpenAI. It's priced at $0.10 per million input tokens and $0.35 per million output tokens at launch, which is 35% cheaper on output than Haiku 5.5 and GPT-6 Luna. Google DeepMind is quietly working on the same architecture with DiffusionGemma, so Inception isn't alone in betting against the autoregressive status quo.

Mercury 3 runs at 1,000 tokens per second, 4x to 8x faster than Anthropic Haiku 5.5 and OpenAI GPT-6 Luna.

Norforge

AI agent hacks 100+ companies for ~$5K

Security firm NorForge found that a single Chinese-speaking hacker used Cairn, an open-source AI agent framework, to breach more than 100 companies after attempting roughly 1,200 sites. The hacker left 20+ control panels exposed without a password, which is how NorForge spotted the operation. Total AI cost was estimated at around $50 per successful breach, putting the full campaign at roughly $5,000 in tokens using Claude Code and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash.

The entire campaign cost roughly $5,000 in AI tokens and successfully breached 100+ companies including Cummins, Deere, and SharkNinja.
