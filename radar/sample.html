Researchers used Claude to hack OpenAI in 72 hours

Security startup Hacktron AI got inside OpenAI's private codebase in under three days this July, using an image-upload bug in OAI's community forum to steal staff tokens that also unlocked ChatGPT accounts. Anthropic's Claude Opus 5 finished writing the attack code within a day of its release, picking up where an earlier model couldn't complete the job. Hacktron reported the breach, left a signed edit on an internal OAI doc as proof, and collected a $6,500 bounty.

A three-person team breached OpenAI's private codebase in under 72 hours and earned a $6,500 bug bounty.
