Openai

OpenAI releases 722 math papers from internal model

OpenAI published 722 manuscripts from an unreleased internal model, grouped into 372 result families, covering pure math, theoretical computer science, and mathematical physics. The boldest claim is a proof of the quasi-Riemann hypothesis, a weaker version of one of math's seven Millennium Prize Problems. The model averaged just three hours of compute per result, and 162 papers have their main results written in Lean, meaning the steps can be machine-verified.

722 manuscripts produced at an average of 3 hours of compute each, with the quasi-Riemann hypothesis among the claimed results.
