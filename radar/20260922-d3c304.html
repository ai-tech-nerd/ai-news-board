#1
Openai

Hacktron used Claude to breach OpenAI in 72 hrs

Security startup Hacktron AI got inside OpenAI's private codebase in under 72 hours this July, with a three-person team and Anthropic's Claude doing a meaningful share of the exploit work. They used an image-upload bug to access OpenAI's community forum, then chained it with a second flaw that let staff sign-in tokens unlock ChatGPT accounts. They left a signed edit on an internal documentation file as proof, then reported it and collected a $6,500 bug bounty.

Three researchers breached OpenAI's private codebase in under 72 hours, earning a $6,500 bug bounty.
