OpenAI's Decisions API clones TypeSafe's Jev model

OpenAI's new Decisions API, announced at DevDay, lets GPT-6 Luna pick from a set of preset answers in roughly 150 milliseconds, optimized for fast structured outputs in agentic workflows. The approach closely mirrors TypeSafe's Jev model, which was released earlier this month. OpenAI's version has the advantage of being baked into the same ecosystem as its frontier models, which gives it distribution Jev can't easily match.

The Decisions API returns structured responses in approximately 150 milliseconds, matching the speed positioning of TypeSafe's Jev model.

#22
Google

Google figures out how to watermark AI proteins

Google has developed a technique to embed watermarks into protein designs created by AI, allowing those designs to be traced back to their origin. This addresses a growing concern in computational biology: as AI generates more novel protein structures, there's no reliable way to identify or audit what's machine-made versus what's naturally occurring or human-designed. The technique is an early step toward provenance tracking in AI-generated scientific content.

Google has developed a working method to embed traceable watermarks into AI-designed protein structures.
