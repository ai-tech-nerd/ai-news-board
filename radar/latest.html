Wharton report: Only 7% of enterprises win with AI

Wharton's fourth annual AI report, based on a survey of 1,416 US enterprise leaders and employees, finds that only 7% of organizations succeed across all three markers of AI success: proving value, transforming workflows, and aligning the organization. The key differentiator isn't how much AI a company uses; it's what they measure. Departments tracking growth metrics like new revenue report strongly positive returns at 35%, versus 13% for those tracking efficiency alone.
