Jensen Huang mulls return; 160K+ sign petition

#2
Openai

OpenAI launches ChatGPT Wallet for agent payments

OpenAI has launched ChatGPT Wallet, a payment system built in partnership with American Express, Mastercard, and Visa that lets its agents make purchases on a user's behalf without exposing actual card numbers. Each transaction swaps card details for a one-time payment token, keeping the underlying data private. It's rolling out first on Dots for US ChatGPT Pro subscribers.

ChatGPT Wallet uses payment tokenization so the user's actual card number is never shared with merchants during agent-initiated purchases.

#3
Anthropic

Claude Duet launches as action-first persistent agent

Anthropic launched Claude Duet in research preview, a persistent background agent that connects to your apps and proactively surfaces tasks it can act on, not just information to be aware of. Where competing agents like OpenAI's Dots might flag a scheduling conflict as a heads-up, Duet typically pairs an alert with an offer to fix it immediately, presenting a Start or Edit button. It's available to Pro, Max, and Ultra plan subscribers who join the waitlist.

Duet's design philosophy centers on judgment: Anthropic says it's meant to decide what needs you, what can wait, and when to check with you first, with safety classifiers running on every draft and file upload.
