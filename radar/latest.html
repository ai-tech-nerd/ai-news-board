Meta Connect 2026: Muse Charm, VR glasses & more

#2
Anthropic

Anthropic's Claude makes first biology discovery

Anthropic's biology lab ran 950 Claude agents for less than a day and came back with something nobody had seen before: a novel enzyme system in bacteria-infecting viruses that looks like CRISPR, the gene-editing technology behind a wave of new medicines. The system, called ART, appears to cut, copy, and paste DNA, but its exact function is still unknown and needs further research. Dario Amodei called the work something he'd have been proud to do as a PhD student.

950 Claude agents ran for 21 hours and consumed 210 million tokens to find the novel ART enzyme system.
