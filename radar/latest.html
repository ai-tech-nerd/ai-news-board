#1
Nvidia

Jensen Huang reinstated as Nvidia CEO after Trump push

Nvidia's board unanimously voted to bring Jensen Huang back as CEO, president, and board member less than two weeks after he resigned over personal conduct issues. President Trump says he personally lobbied the board, with allies arguing Huang was too important to US AI competitiveness to lose. Huang returns while still facing a congressional hearing in December, and the board chair appointment from his resignation period stays in place.

Nvidia stock jumped over 31% on the news, adding more than $1 trillion in market value in a single trading session.

#2
Openai

OpenAI builds 'Athena' semantic layer for agents

OpenAI is developing an internal data management platform called Athena to help its enterprise agents better understand business data. The platform works like a semantic layer, giving agents standardized definitions for metrics like revenue and customers so they don't have to guess at context from raw data. It's early-stage with no release timeline, but it's a direct response to accuracy problems that surfaced when at least one enterprise customer found the existing agent wasn't reliable enough for broad deployment.

At least one enterprise customer found OpenAI's business data agent isn't yet accurate enough for widespread use.

Colgate-palmolive

LLMs simulate consumer research at ~90% accuracy

Colgate-Palmolive researchers tested whether LLMs could stand in for real survey respondents and found that their Semantic Similarity Rating technique hit about 90% reliability compared to how real humans answer when they retake the same survey. The test covered 9,300 actual human responses. It's not a replacement for real research, but it's close enough to be a serious augmentation tool.
