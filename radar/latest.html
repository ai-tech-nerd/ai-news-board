OpenAI models disprove Exponential Time Hypothesis

OpenAI says its unreleased models have disproved the Exponential Time Hypothesis, a decades-old assumption underpinning hundreds of papers in theoretical computer science, and the original proponents of the hypothesis are reviewing the claim. The same models also proved a 35-year-old wave conjecture and part of a $1M Millennium Prize problem. All three proofs were computer-verified before the announcement.
