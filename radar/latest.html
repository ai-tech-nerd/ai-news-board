Amazon / meta

Amazon blocks Meta Muse from shopping

Amazon cut off Meta's Muse agent from its platform just 12 days after launch, accusing it of browsing without identifying itself and appearing to store customer credentials. Meta disputes the credential claim, saying Muse uses secure storage and can't view passwords or payment details. The real stakes aren't just about security: Muse-powered purchases would bypass Amazon's sponsored listings, the engine behind a $56B ad business.

Amazon's ad business generates an estimated $56B annually, revenue that agent-driven shopping would route around by skipping sponsored listings entirely.
