Gartner: Agentic AI Inference Costs Will Rise 5x by 2028

Research firm Gartner predicts that AI inference costs per agentic workflow will increase more than fivefold by 2028, driven by an 'inference paradox' where efficiency gains lead to more complex and token-hungry applications rather than lower costs. Enterprises face top-down pressure to adopt AI agents despite unpredictable returns, while compute constraints continue to tighten. Experts warn that companies are adopting AI without fully understanding total cost of ownership.

Gartner predicts AI inference costs per agentic workflow will increase more than 5x through 2028, even as unit economics improve.

#8

AI May Deplete the 'Cognitive Commons' of Human Expertise

A new academic paper argues that widespread AI adoption could slowly erode humanity's collective knowledge base by replacing the entry-level workers who eventually become tomorrow's experts. Applying the 'Tragedy of the Commons' economic framework, the paper warns that individual firms maximizing profit by automating junior roles will collectively reduce the supply of qualified professionals capable of overseeing AI systems a decade from now. The core question raised: who will verify AI's work when the human experts are gone?

The paper applies the Tragedy of the Commons framework to AI: individual firms automating entry-level roles collectively deplete the future supply of human experts needed to oversee AI.
