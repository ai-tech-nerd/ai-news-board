Runway

Runway's Solaris: AI-Rendered Interfaces Without Code

Runway introduced Solaris, an 'Interface World Model' that renders websites and apps as live video in real time, with every frame drawn as a user interacts — no code running underneath. The system pairs Runway's Gen-4.5 video model with an LLM that interprets each click or drag and prompts the next frames. In user testing, Solaris was preferred over pages coded by Claude Opus 5 in 71% of matchups on in-scene behavior.

AI ECG Model Detects Heart Disease in Two Seconds

Imperial College London researchers developed an AI model that analyzes routine ECGs in under two seconds, identifying heart failure and valve disease that doctors typically cannot detect without ultrasound. Trained on 10.6 million ECGs and tested on 65,000 patients, the model flagged heart failure in 81% of cases and valve disease in 90%. A 590-patient trial across six hospitals is underway, with a goal of NHS integration within two years.

The AI model detected valve disease successfully in 90% of cases, trained on 10.6M ECGs
